E. About the data

This undirected, simple graph joins two airports when the OpenFlights snapshot records a zero-stop route in either direction. Only airports with country exactly “United States” and a valid three-letter IATA code qualify. The map and lookup show the airports with at least one domestic edge; airports with zero domestic routes are counted separately. Multiple airlines and directions collapse into one edge. Alaska and Hawaii are included; territories listed under their own country names are excluded by this filter. These historical routes cannot establish current flight availability.

Source: OpenFlights airport and route data · Open Database License (ODbL) · State boundaries: PublicaMundi / U.S. Census.
